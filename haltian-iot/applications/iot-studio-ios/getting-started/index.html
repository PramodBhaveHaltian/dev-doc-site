Getting Started

Installation, logging in, and navigation basics for Haltian IoT Studio iOS app

This guide walks through installing Haltian IoT Studio on your iOS device and understanding the basic navigation.

Installation

Download via TestFlight

The app is currently available through Apple TestFlight for beta testing.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store (if not already installed)
  2. Open this link on your iPhone or iPad: https://testflight.apple.com/join/ZlZYgdLc
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions to install both TestFlight and Haltian IoT Studio
  4. Wait for installation to complete

Note: TestFlight apps expire after 90 days. You will receive a notification to update when a new version is available.

System Requirements

  • Device: iPhone or iPad
  • iOS Version: iOS 15.0 or later
  • Network: Internet connection required
  • Optional: Camera for QR scanning, NFC capability for tag setup

Logging In

Roles: All users

  1. Launch the Haltian IoT Studio app
  2. Enter your email address and password
  3. Tap Sign In
  4. Wait for the app to load your organization data

The app will automatically attempt to sign in using saved credentials on subsequent launches.

Tip: If auto-login fails, you will be returned to the login screen to re-enter your credentials.

Main Navigation

After logging in, you’ll see the main interface with a sidebar on the left and content area on the right.

Sidebar Sections

The sidebar is organized into the following sections:

SectionPurpose
InventoryFind and browse devices
SetupInstall devices and configure sites
DevicesManage keywords, alerts, groups, and device configuration
AccountUser settings and administration
OtherAdditional tools and information
ExperimentalFeatures in development (if enabled)

Navigation Tips

  • Tap a sidebar item to view that section
  • Pull down on lists to refresh data
  • Swipe left on list items for quick actions (delete, etc.)
  • Use the back button to return to previous screens
  • On iPad, toggle sidebar visibility using the sidebar button

Understanding User Roles

Your role determines what features you can access:

RoleKey Capabilities
ViewerView devices and measurements (read-only)
InstallerInstall devices, assign keywords, edit properties
DesignerCreate/modify spaces, floors, zones, floor plans
ManagerFull access including user management and exports
SysadminOrganization management and data retention

To check your role:

  1. Open the sidebar
  2. Navigate to Account > Own settings
  3. View your Organization role
