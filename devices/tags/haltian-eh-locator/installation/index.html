Installation

Complete installation guide for Haltian EH Locator including indoor light mounting, cable shelf installation, wall mounting, power requirements, and LED diagnostics

Installation Overview

The Haltian EH Locator offers flexible installation options to suit various environments. This guide covers three primary mounting methods:

  1. Indoor Light Mount - Attach to ceiling light fixtures using energy harvesting
  2. Cable Shelf Mount - Mount on cable management or ceiling infrastructure with USB-C power
  3. Wall Mount - Install on walls at recommended height with USB-C power
Before Installation

Ensure the Haltian IoT Gateway is operational and connected before installing EH Locators. Locators require an active Wirepas mesh network to function properly.

Pre-Installation Planning

Network Coverage Planning

For optimal tracking and positioning performance:

  • Locator Density: Install sufficient locators to ensure complete area coverage
  • Spacing: Follow recommended spacing guidelines (see Glossary for “Locator Spacing”)
  • Line of Sight: Maximize RF communication paths between locators and tags
  • Height: Install at consistent heights for positioning accuracy

Things to Avoid

❌ Do NOT:

  • Install locators only on opposite sides of a shared wall (creates RF shadow)
  • Block devices with metal surfaces (prevents radio communication)
  • Create excessive distances between locators, tags, or gateway
  • Install near high-power RF transmitters (WiFi routers, etc.)
  • Expose to wet conditions (dripping, splashing, or spraying water)
  • Install in excessively dusty environments
  • Exceed recommended operating temperature (+50°C)
  • Install on greasy or dirty surfaces

✅ Do:

  • Plan locator placement for overlapping coverage
  • Ensure adequate spacing per guidelines
  • Keep RF paths clear of metal obstructions
  • Maintain recommended environmental conditions

Installation Method 1: Indoor Light Mount

Perfect for warehouses, offices, and facilities with bright overhead lighting. This method uses energy harvesting only (no cables).

Equipment Needed

  • Haltian EH Locator
  • Mounting bracket (included)
  • Zip ties (included)
  • Lux meter (recommended, for verifying light levels)

Step-by-Step Installation

1. Verify Light Levels

Before installation, measure lux levels at the mounting height:

Lux LevelRouter: Required Light Hours/Week
40,000+ lux35+ hours (ideal)
30,000 lux50+ hours
20,000 lux85+ hours
15,000 lux168 hours (always on)
<10,000 luxInsufficient - use USB-C power
Light Measurement

Measure lux at the exact height where the locator will be installed. Office lighting typically provides 300-500 lux at desk level, but 10,000-40,000+ lux directly under ceiling fixtures.

2. Install the Mounting Bracket

  1. Position the bracket on the light fixture or ceiling mount
  2. Thread zip ties through bracket mounting holes
  3. Secure bracket tightly to light fixture
  4. Ensure bracket is level and stable

3. Power On the Locator

  1. Locate the power switch on the EH Locator
  2. Slide switch to ON position
  3. Power LED should blink green (device powered on)

4. Attach Locator to Bracket

  1. Align the locator with the bracket mounting points
  2. Press firmly until securely attached
  3. Ensure the solar panel faces downward toward the light source
Solar Panel Positioning

Do NOT block any part of the solar panel during installation. The entire panel must be exposed to light for optimal energy harvesting.

5. Verify Power and Network Status

Immediately after installation (first 10 minutes):

  • Power LED: Should show constant green when indoor lights are on
    • If blinking or off: Insufficient light - relocate or add USB-C power
  • Network LED: Should change from blinking to constant green (router) or blue (beacon)
    • Blinking green: Still establishing mesh connection (wait 1-2 minutes)
    • Constant green: Router mode active, mesh connected ✓
    • Constant blue: Beacon mode active ✓
    • Blinking red: Error - check troubleshooting section
LED Auto-Off

After 10 minutes of successful operation, LEDs automatically turn off to conserve power. This is normal behavior.

Indoor Light Mount - Complete

The locator is now operational using energy harvesting. Monitor battery status in Haltian IoT Studio to ensure sufficient charging.

Installation Method 2: Cable Shelf Mount

Ideal for facilities with cable management infrastructure or false ceilings. This method uses USB-C power for reliable continuous operation.

Equipment Needed

  • Haltian EH Locator
  • Mounting bracket (included)
  • Zip ties (included)
  • USB-C power cable
  • USB-C power adapter (5V) or PoE-USB-C adapter

Step-by-Step Installation

1. Install the Mounting Bracket

  1. Position bracket on cable shelf or ceiling rail
  2. Thread zip ties through bracket mounting holes
  3. Secure bracket to shelf/rail infrastructure
  4. Verify bracket stability

2. Power On the Locator

  1. Slide power switch to ON position
  2. Power LED should blink green

3. Attach Locator to Bracket

  1. Align locator with bracket
  2. Ensure LED indicators point straight downward
    • Antenna pattern optimized toward monitored zone
    • Improves tag communication and positioning accuracy
  3. Press firmly to secure

4. Connect USB-C Power

  1. Connect USB-C cable to locator
  2. Connect other end to power adapter or PoE-USB-C adapter
  3. Route cable neatly along ceiling infrastructure
  4. Power LED should show constant green (powered)
Power Accessibility

Ensure the power outlet is easily accessible for maintenance and troubleshooting.

5. Verify Network Establishment

Watch the Network LED:

  • Initial: Blinking green (establishing mesh)
  • After 1-2 minutes: Constant green (router) or blue (beacon)
  • If blinking red: Check troubleshooting section

Cable Shelf Mount - Complete

The locator is now operational with continuous USB-C power.

Installation Method 3: Wall Mount

Suitable for offices, corridors, and areas without ceiling access. This method uses USB-C power.

Equipment Needed

  • Haltian EH Locator
  • Mounting bracket (included, optional)
  • Sticky tape (included)
  • USB-C power cable and adapter
  • Level (recommended)

Step-by-Step Installation

1. Select Installation Location

Height requirements:

  • Minimum height: 2.5m (7.5 feet) from floor
  • Recommended height: 2.5-3m for optimal coverage

Positioning:

  • Clear line of sight to monitored area
  • Avoid metal surfaces or obstructions nearby
  • Accessible for LED verification and maintenance

2. Prepare Wall Surface

  1. Clean installation area with dry cloth
  2. Remove dust, grease, or debris
  3. Ensure surface is dry and smooth
  4. Mark installation position with pencil (optional)

3. Power On the Locator

  1. Slide power switch to ON position
  2. Verify green LED blinks (powered on)

4. Mount the Locator

Option A: With Bracket (recommended for heavier devices or temporary installations)

  1. Attach sticky tape to back of bracket
  2. Attach locator to bracket
  3. Remove tape backing
  4. Press firmly to wall for 10 seconds
  5. Hold until adhesive sets

Option B: Direct Mounting (simpler, fewer components)

  1. Attach sticky tape directly to back of locator
  2. Remove tape backing
  3. Use level to ensure device is straight
  4. Press firmly to wall for 10 seconds
Tape Adhesion

The sticky tape provides strong adhesion to clean, smooth surfaces. For rough or textured walls, use the bracket with zip ties around a structural element if possible.

5. Connect USB-C Power

  1. Connect USB-C cable to locator
  2. Route cable to nearest power outlet
  3. Connect to power adapter
  4. Secure cable with cable clips if needed (not included)
  5. Power LED should show constant green

6. Verify Network Connection

  • Network LED should transition to constant green (router) or blue (beacon)
  • If blinking red after 2-3 minutes, check troubleshooting

Wall Mount - Complete

  • Monitor Power and Network LEDs
  • Verify correct colors and patterns
  • Troubleshoot any red or incorrect states

  • This is normal power-saving behavior
  • Device continues operating normally
  • Check Haltian IoT Studio for status updates

Post-Installation Verification

1. Check Haltian IoT Studio

  1. Log in to Haltian IoT Studio
  2. Navigate to Devices section
  3. Locate the EH Locator by QR code/TUID
  4. Verify:
    • ✓ Device shows as online
    • ✓ Battery level is displayed
    • ✓ Power source indicated (solar/USB-C)
    • ✓ Operating mode (Router/Beacon) is correct
    • ✓ Recent messages received

2. Verify Network Connectivity

In IoT Studio, check:

  • RSSI (signal strength): Should be > -85 dBm
  • Message count: Should increase over time
  • Mesh hops: Number of hops to gateway (lower is better)

3. Test Tag Communication

If using with asset tags:

  1. Place a Haltian NANO Tag or compatible tag near the locator
  2. Verify tag appears in IoT Studio
  3. Check that tag messages are received via the locator
  4. Monitor positioning data if in beacon mode

Troubleshooting

Power LED Issues

Problem: Power LED is off or blinking (when using indoor light harvesting)

Solutions:

  • Measure lux levels at installation height
  • Relocate to brighter area (closer to light source)
  • Switch to USB-C power if light is insufficient
  • Check that solar panel is not blocked

Problem: Power LED off when using USB-C

Solutions:

  • Verify USB-C cable is fully inserted
  • Test power adapter with another device
  • Check power outlet functionality
  • Try different USB-C cable
  • Verify power switch is in ON position

Network LED Issues

Problem: Network LED blinking red

Possible causes and solutions:

  1. Network Error

    • Verify gateway is online and operational
    • Check that locator is within mesh network range
    • Reduce distance to nearest locator or gateway
    • Check for RF obstructions (metal surfaces)

  2. Battery Low

    • Connect USB-C power immediately
    • If using solar, verify sufficient light exposure
    • Allow time for battery to recharge
    • Monitor battery level in IoT Studio

  3. Undefined Error

    • Power cycle device (off/on)
    • Check firmware version in IoT Studio
    • Contact support if persistent

Problem: Network LED stays blinking green for >5 minutes

Solutions:

  • Verify gateway is powered on and connected
  • Reduce distance to gateway or another locator
  • Check for RF interference sources
  • Power cycle device and wait 2-3 minutes

Device Not Appearing in IoT Studio

Solutions:

  • Verify device was properly provisioned to your organization
  • Check that gateway is online and connected
  • Wait 5-10 minutes for initial discovery
  • Power cycle the locator
  • Verify QR code matches device in Studio
  • Contact support for device provisioning

Installation Checklist

Use this checklist for each EH Locator installation:

  • Gateway operational and connected to cloud
  • Installation location planned (mounting method selected)
  • Light levels verified (if using energy harvesting) or USB-C power available
  • Mounting surface cleaned and prepared
  • Device unpacked and QR code recorded
  • Mounting bracket installed (if applicable)
  • Power switch turned ON
  • Locator securely mounted to bracket/wall
  • USB-C power connected (if applicable)
  • Power LED verified (constant green)
  • Network LED verified (constant green or blue after 2 min)
  • Device appears in Haltian IoT Studio (within 10 min)
  • Operating mode confirmed (Router/Beacon)
  • Battery status checked in IoT Studio
  • Signal strength acceptable (RSSI > -85 dBm)
  • Tag communication verified (if applicable)
  • Installation documented (location, TUID, mounting method)

Configuration After Installation

Setting Operating Mode

The EH Locator can operate as a Router or Beacon. Configure via Haltian IoT Studio:

  1. Navigate to device in IoT Studio
  2. Select Configuration tab
  3. Choose operating mode:
    • Router: For mesh networking and message forwarding
    • Beacon: For positioning reference only
  4. Adjust beacon interval if needed (default: 1 second)
  5. Save configuration
  6. Device will update remotely

Network LED will change:

  • Router mode: Green
  • Beacon mode: Blue

Firmware Updates

EH Locators can be updated remotely via Haltian IoT Studio:

  1. Navigate to Firmware section in IoT Studio
  2. Select devices to update
  3. Choose target firmware version
  4. Initiate update
  5. Monitor update progress
  6. Verify devices come back online after update

Maintenance

Regular Maintenance Tasks

Monthly (if using energy harvesting):

  • Check battery level in IoT Studio
  • Verify power harvesting status
  • Clean solar panel if dusty (dry cloth only)

Quarterly:

  • Verify device online status
  • Check RSSI signal strength
  • Review message delivery statistics
  • Update firmware if new version available

Annually:

  • Physical inspection of mounting
  • Verify LEDs function during power-on
  • Test battery backup (disconnect USB-C temporarily)
  • Clean device exterior if needed

Battery Replacement

The internal Li-ion battery is designed for:

  • Router mode: Backup power during outages (10 days max)
  • Beacon mode: 4 years of operation (if energy harvesting supplements)
Battery Replacement

The internal battery is not user-replaceable. Contact Haltian support if battery replacement is needed. The device is designed for minimal maintenance with proper power management.

Advanced Installation Scenarios

High-Ceiling Installations

For warehouses or facilities with ceilings >5m:

  • Use extension poles for mounting bracket installation
  • Ensure adequate light levels at mounting height (measure before installation)
  • Consider using USB-C with PoE adapter for reliable power
  • Plan for higher ladder/lift requirements for maintenance

Low-Light Environments

For storage areas, basements, or 24/7 dark areas:

  • Always use USB-C power (energy harvesting insufficient)
  • Consider PoE-USB-C adapters for centralized power management
  • Plan cable routing before installation
  • Use cable management to secure power cables

Outdoor-Adjacent Installations

For loading docks, covered outdoor areas, or skylighted zones:

  • Verify temperature stays within 0°C to +50°C
  • Ensure IP40 rating is adequate (no direct water exposure)
  • Monitor for condensation in high-humidity environments
  • Consider solar exposure levels (may be higher than indoor)

Temporary Installations

For events, construction sites, or pilot projects:

  • Use mounting bracket with removable adhesive or zip ties
  • Prefer USB-C power for easy relocation
  • Document installation positions for future reference
  • Plan for easy removal without damage to surfaces

Safety Guidelines

  • Electrical Safety: Only use approved 5V USB-C power adapters
  • Height Safety: Use appropriate ladders/lifts for ceiling installations
  • RF Exposure: Device operates at +4 dBm max TX power (well below safety limits)
  • Disposal: Follow WEEE guidelines for electronic waste disposal
  • Fire Safety: Do not install near heat sources exceeding +50°C
